Privacy Policy – Chinese Chess

This privacy policy applies to the Chinese Chess app for mobile devices (the "Application"), published by Milaan (the "Service Provider").

Information we collect

The Application is free and does not require an account. It does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos or any other personal data that identifies you. Game progress, statistics and settings are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the Application.

Analytics

The Application uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how players use it, so the Service Provider can fix problems and improve the game. Firebase may automatically collect information such as an app instance ID, the advertising ID, device model, operating system and app version, country or approximate region, and usage events (for example app opens, games started and completed, and session length). This information is aggregated and is not used to identify you personally. It is processed by Google under its own terms:

  • Privacy and Security in Firebase

The Service Provider does not sell this information and does not share it with third parties other than Google as described here.

Advertising

The Application is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may automatically collect information from your device, such as the advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system and approximate location derived from the IP address. This collection is done by Google under its own privacy policy, not by the Service Provider, and the Service Provider does not receive information that identifies you.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later in the Application. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Android: Settings › Google › Ads; iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking).

Children

The Application is not directed at children under 13. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

Data retention and deletion

Analytics data is kept by Firebase for the retention period set by the Service Provider (no longer than 14 months) and then deleted. To request deletion of analytics data linked to your device, contact us at the address below. Uninstalling the Application stops all further collection.

Security

Data collected by Firebase and AdMob is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored by Google. The Service Provider does not run its own servers holding your data.

Changes
