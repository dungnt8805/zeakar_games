Privacy Policy – Jigsaw Puzzle Kids

This privacy policy applies to the Jigsaw Puzzle Kids app for mobile devices (the "Application"), published by Milaan (the "Service Provider"). The Application is designed for children and their families, and we take children's privacy seriously.

Information we collect

The Application is free and does not require an account. It does not ask for or collect a name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information that identifies a child or parent. There is no chat, no sharing with other players and no way to post content. Puzzle progress and settings are stored only on the device and are deleted when the Application is uninstalled.

No advertising

The Application contains no advertising of any kind. It does not use the advertising ID, does not track children across other apps or websites, and does not share any information with advertising networks.

Analytics

The Application uses Google Firebase Analytics only to understand, in aggregate, how the Application is used, so the Service Provider can fix problems and improve the puzzles. Firebase may automatically collect non-personal technical information such as an app instance ID, device model, operating system and app version, country or approximate region, and usage events (for example app opens, puzzles started and completed, and session length). Firebase Analytics is configured with advertising ID collection and ad personalization turned off. This information is not used to identify, contact or profile any child, and it is not used for advertising. It is processed by Google under its own terms:

  • Privacy and Security in Firebase

The Service Provider does not sell this information and does not share it with third parties other than Google as described here.

Children's privacy

The Application is intended for children and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR and the Google Play Families Policy. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

Data retention and deletion

Analytics data is kept by Firebase for the retention period set by the Service Provider (no longer than 14 months) and then deleted. Parents can request deletion of analytics data linked to their device by contacting us at the address below. Uninstalling the Application stops all further collection.

Security

Data collected by Firebase is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored by Google. The Service Provider does not run its own servers holding any user data.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date.
