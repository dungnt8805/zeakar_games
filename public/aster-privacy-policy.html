Online accounts, friends and co-op

Opening Friends creates an online account through Firebase Authentication, using a Google Play Games player ID when available or an anonymous account otherwise. We process account identifiers and authentication tokens to sign you in and secure requests. Anonymous accounts are not automatically linked to Play Games and may become inaccessible after reinstalling the app.

We store your display name, selected built-in avatar, friend code, private deletion key, ship and skin IDs, weapon upgrade levels, profile revisions and activity timestamps. We also store friend requests, friendships, blocks, name reports, assist reward records and counters used to prevent duplicate rewards and abuse. These records are processed using Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions for Firebase to provide and protect social features.

Players who enter your friend code or exchange invitations can see your display name, avatar and player identifier. Accepted friends can also receive your ship and weapon snapshot for AI-controlled assistance. Co-op uses asynchronous snapshots rather than a live multiplayer session. We do not upload your address book, voice recordings or personal photos for this feature. Keep your deletion key private; it is not part of your shared profile.


In-app purchases

Google Play processes payments. Our verification service processes purchase tokens, product and order identifiers, installation and account identifiers, transaction times, purchase status and refund or revocation information to grant or restore purchases and prevent fraud. We do not receive your payment card details. Purchase verification records are separate from the social account and may be retained for fraud prevention, refunds and legal obligations after social account deletion.


Advertising, analytics and diagnostics

AdMob may collect and share IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, advertising or other device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics process gameplay events, device and app information, crash reports and diagnostics when usage sharing is enabled. Gameplay events may include the number of friend assists, but we do not attach social account identifiers to analytics or crash reports.

You can turn off Share usage data in Settings to disable our Firebase analytics and crash collection. This control does not disable the data processing needed for ads, purchases or online friends. Advertising choices are managed through the available consent options and your device advertising settings.


Deleting your online account

In the game, open Settings, then Online account, then Delete account. A successful request deletes the online account immediately. You can also request deletion without reinstalling the game at:

Account deletion request

The web form asks for your friend code, an optional private deletion key, and optional contact email and note. Web requests wait 30 days before deletion, including requests with a valid deletion key. You can cancel a pending request from Friends in the game. We process the submitted information and IP-based rate limits to handle requests and prevent abuse. Deletion request records, including any contact email, are removed after 90 days following processing.

Deletion removes the Firebase Authentication account, profile, friend code, private deletion key, friendships, invitations, your reward inbox and associated social counters, and clears the local social cache and tokens when deletion is completed in the app. Online account and social records are retained while the account exists and are subject to this deletion process rather than the general User Provided Data period below.

Deletion does not erase local game progress, Google Play Games cloud saves, purchase verification records or independent advertising and diagnostic data. Manage local data through your device and cloud saves through Play Games. Rewards already granted to others remain; their sender field is replaced, but record identifiers may retain the former account identifier. Other players may retain that identifier in their block lists for safety. Analytics and crash records follow the relevant service retention settings and are not linked by us to the social account.


Local storage

The app stores authentication tokens, cached friend profiles, selected allies and pending rewards on your device so that it can reconnect and provide offline assistance. Cached snapshots can remain on a friend’s device until refreshed. Removing the app does not delete server-side account data; use the account deletion options above.
